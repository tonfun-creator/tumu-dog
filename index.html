❓ FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What is TUMU DOG?

A cute, food-loving meme community on Solana built around Japanese food, wholesome vibes and internet culture.

Where can I buy $TUMU?

$TUMU is not live yet. At launch you can buy only through the official Pump.fun button on this website. Follow our X and Telegram for the announcement.

What is the trading pair?

SOL, on the Solana network.

How do the rewards work?

Ranks 1–10 can receive 100 USDC and ranks 11–100 can receive 25 USDC, with a minimum qualifying purchase of 25 USDC. Final rules, dates and verification will be published before launch.

What is the contract address?

It will be posted on this website and on our official X account @TUMUDOG1. Never trust an address from anyone else.

How does Pump.fun liquidity and graduation work?

$TUMU starts on the Pump.fun bonding curve (1B total supply). When about 85 SOL is collected, the token graduates and its liquidity moves to PumpSwap. At graduation the pool tokens are locked and burned, so nobody can pull the liquidity manually.

Is $TUMU a safe investment?

No guarantees. $TUMU is a meme coin, not financial advice, and the price can go to zero. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

How do I stay safe from scams?

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Admins will never DM you first asking for funds. Only trust links on this site and our official X.

Where can I contact the team?

Message us on X (@TUMUDOG1) or Telegram. Links are in the Follow TUMU section.
